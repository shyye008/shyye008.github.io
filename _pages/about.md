<h1>Shangyuan Ye <span class="chinese" lang="zh">叶上元</span></h1>
<p class="lead">Assistant Professor of Statistics</p>
<div class="intro"><p>I am an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Florida International University. My research focuses on statistical methods for high-dimensional data analysis, clustered data analysis, and nonparametric statistics.</p>
<p>Before joining FIU, I was an Assistant Staff Scientist at the Knight Cancer Institute Biostatistics Shared Resource at Oregon Health &amp; Science University. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Oklahoma State University in 2018 and completed postdoctoral training at UMass Medical School and Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute &amp; Harvard Medical School.</p></div>
